## Task

Do tasks 1-5 of "Phase K0". There is no game code, no flag and no build.

**June's loader, in Ghidra** (`ghidra` skill, read-only):
- Search for the `KIM ` magic `0x204d494b`, the `0x1201` compare, and `*0xe4` / `imul ..,0xe4` index arithmetic.
- Record these, as funcmap rows tagged `KIM K0`:
  - June's `DoMemoryNotEncrypt` equivalent, and whether June added a check;
  - `GetItemTemplet`;
  - the set-table reader;
  - `ResetItemManager`.

**The 228-byte map:**
- For every June templet accessor, record the offset, type and June address.
- Name each of the 40 new bytes from its xrefs. The planning session's guesses are in "Established facts" and are not evidence.
- Answer these four:
  - What does `flags2` bit 2 mean?
  - What is item type 14? Use `june_src_fixed/data036_kom/Enum.src.lua` for this.
  - What width do the Extra per-unit-type entries have?
  - Did `MAX_MODEL_COUNT_A_ITEM` change, and is the set record still 20 B?

**Routing.**
- Route every new field to one of:
  - port in K2;
  - a backlog stage (11, 12, 8, or a new stage, following the rule that an unowned June branch gets its own phase);
  - the character-port checklist.
- Also route June's `SetItemGroup.lua` / `g_pCX2SetItemManager` and `ItemManager.lua` (`SetItemLock_*`).
- Record how June chooses `item_LA.kim` / `SetItem_LA.lua`, as a one-line note only.

**`tools/kim.py`:**
- Read-only, stdlib only.
- Detect the stride from the header and the id table: 188 is March, 228 is June, anything else is an error.
- Subcommands: `dump`, `diff`, `census` and `assets`.
- The June offsets come from your map.
- Add a line to `tools/README.md`.

**Done means:**

  `june2017_re/funcmap.tsv`.
