## Task

The C9h-4b review found that June's device-manager `PlaySound` `31ce0e10` takes a 4th argument. When it is 1 and the call has just opened the
device, it registers an FMOD channel-end callback through `31cdd4e0` (callback `31cdcca0`). At channel end, `31cdcbb0` queues the device
(count 1, matching channel) in the sound manager's `+0x240` list, and the frame move `31cde8d0` deletes it through `31cdd8d0`. March has
no such callback, so a device opened by `PlaySound` stays cached. 174 of 242 June call sites pass 1. The other `31cdd4e0` caller is
`32800e10`; identify it.

1. **Map it fully (read-only).**
   - What the 4th parameter is in June's `CKTDXDeviceManager` (name, default), from the call-site census: 1 / 0 / register.
   - The 9 sites that pass 0, and what they do with the handle. The expectation is that they keep it. Check it.
   - Whether anything else drains `+0x240` or bumps a device's count.
2. **Audit March.** List every caller of `CKTDXDeviceManager::PlaySound` that **stores** the returned handle: members, vectors, the
   square's `m_vecSoundToDeleteOnStateEnd`. With June's auto-release, each of those handles can dangle once its sound ends. Give each
   one's June counterpart and the argument June passes there. JUNE_BUGS 122 (the square) is the known case.
3. **Stop and hand the decision to the user, with the audit.** The choices:
   - (a) keep March's cached devices. Nothing is ported, and the C9h-4b port stays as it is.
   - (b) port June's auto-release with June's per-site arguments. The sites June leaves exposed, like the square, keep June's bug
     verbatim and get JUNE_BUGS rows.
   - (c) port it, but pass 0 wherever March stores the handle.

   Copying June verbatim can put a use-after-free into March, so this is the "ask" case of the June-bugs rule, not a silent port.

**Done gate (read-only outcome):** funcmap rows for every piece, the audit as a `.tsv`, and the user's answer recorded in the Done note.
If the answer is (b) or (c), the port lands in the same phase or a follow-up row: the user decides that.
