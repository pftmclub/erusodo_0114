## Amended by R0 (2026-10-22)

Your rows in `june2017_re/result_r0_map.tsv` have target `R1`; `R1/R3` rows are shared with R3. What R0 settled:

- **Owner and load.** Vslot 0xb8 `324c8b80` does `new CX2UIDungeonResultAdapter( movie )`, where the movie is the GFx manager's
  `+0x98` vslot 1, or 0 when there is no manager. The adapter goes into state `+0x7c4`. That makes the owner the **dungeon state**,
  not `CX2DungeonGame`; put the member on `CX2StateDungeonGame`, which training inherits.
  - The widget opens with `openUI( 8 )` `31c44530`, tests with `isOpenUI` `31c44710` and closes with `closeUI` `31c445d0`. All three are
    june-gfx G2 wrappers and already ported.
  - The bindings use the G3 data-binding wrapper (`31d1f900` add, `31d1ef00` find, `31d1ee10` notify, `31d1f600` remove).
  - No IME or resize piece is involved.
- **Port whole:**
  - the ctor `32eea7e0` with its 16 bindings and their value classes (`3353a0b0` 0x88 B, `3353a080` 0x40 B, `334c9084` bool, `33471e28`);
  - the dtor `32eea090` / `32eeafd0`, the item setter `32eea6a0`, `32eea1c0` show / `32eea1f0` enable retry;
  - `updateDungeonInfo` `32eeae30` and the to-AS function `32eeb1d0`, whose member names are in the tsv;
  - the item to-AS `32eeaff0`;
  - the callback table `33539ea8` (5 entries; keep June's `notfy` spelling);
  - vslots 0xb8 / 0xbc / 0xc4 / 0xc8 / 0xcc, the fill vslot 0xc0 `324d60e0` (struct `324e20d0`), the item fill `324cf8b0` and the
    arrows `324d1210` / `324d1270`.
- **Values.** The tsv's "updateDungeonInfo members" block gives each member's June source and March source. Several values are
  June-only and keep June's absent value, which is the branch June takes when the data is missing:
  - `hitRank`, `rewardType`, `bestSec`, `resonanceLevel` are 0;
  - the exp / ED bonus string lists are empty;
  - category 1, 4 and 5 detail values are 0.

  Do **not** fill them from March's bonus fields.
- **Items.** These come from the dungeon game's obtained-item list (`+0xdf4`, 0xc0 B records, vslots 0x21c / 0x220 / 0x224 / 0x228),
  which March does not have. Locate the 0x21c caller first (R0 could not: it is a virtual call, probably the get-item NOT path), then
  port the list with it. The March data is `m_mapItemObtained` (`KEGS_UPDATE_UNIT_INFO_NOT`).
