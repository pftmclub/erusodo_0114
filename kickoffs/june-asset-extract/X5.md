**From the X4 review (plan doc "Phase X4 - Review"):**
- **No loose long-name files in the game dir.** `LoadRealFile` and `LoadTwoRealFiles` `StrCpyW` the caller's name unbounded into
  the 60-char `strFileName`, and loose files are tried before the koms. Never drop a loose copy of a long-name asset to test it.
  This is pre-existing and not X4's.
- **Reverting X4's flag after this phase means reverting data048 and data197 too.** With the flag off, long names fall back to
  their 59-char cut key, and `NOT_ALLOW_DUPLICATE_FILES` rejects a whole kom on a collision. Say so in the Done note.
- **If a long-name asset loads with no `found` line,** look for a caller-side name field that cuts the name before it reaches the
  device (the June EffectSet `.tga` field, the sound table's `.ogg` field). The engine side has no buffer under 100 characters.
