## Lessons from D1-3b / D1-3c (2026-10-29)

- Read the per-frame function's disassembly, not the decompile, to map stack slots to handles.
- A March block under an undefined studio flag is enabled by widening its guard to `#if defined( <studio flag> ) || defined( <June flag> )`
  (flagcheck reports one ERROR row per such line: explain them), or, for a whole feature the global build `#undef`s, by re-defining the studio
  flag at the end of `Always.h` under the June flag (D1-3c, proven with `cl /EP` on `KTDX.h` and `X2Lib/StdAfx.h`).
- No play-test hand-off: the plan's smoke runs once at the end. Finish = flagcheck, build, DEPLOY OK, Done note, commit.
