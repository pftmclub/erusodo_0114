What Phase 0 found (details are in "Phase 0 - Done"):
- **June's client never reads `MONSTER_GRADE` from a stage script** (`327eb8e0`). The grade
  arrives only by packet (`327edb60`). Reading it in the shared `FetchNPCData` is therefore
  the emulator playing the server, for `BuildStageNpcData`. The game's NPCs must still take
  their grade from `AddNPCData`, never from the client's own parse.
- **June never calls `SetMonsterGrade` on the dungeon path** (`327574c0` has zero callers).
  Do not call it.
- **Set the ExtraNPCStat grade whenever the NPC ID has a row** (`HasExtraNPCStat`), not only
  inside the base-only (no legacy row) stat branch. June writes `+0xdc` and scan/near for
  every row ID (`32384daa`). Gating on the base-only branch cuts the reach from 66 bosses to 4.

  reads scan/near). Prove that March's `SetHardLevel` read (`X2GUNPC.cpp:7210`) happens after
  your setter.
