What Phase 0 found (details are in "Phase 0 - Done", items 5-6):
- **Attribute:** on dungeons it is a bool (`IsAttribNpc`, from `AttribNpcTable.lua`, a
  server-only file). Offline never spawns attributed NPCs, so the attribute branch is always
  false. Port the check as written, with that input false, and add no attribute system.
- **Item lottery: no factor.** `fDungeonFactor` and `m_bIsBoss` never reach it
  (`KDropTable.cpp:1291-1358`). Apply the factor to EXP and ED only.
- **70057 / 70058** ("Essence of Life", the small and big HP orbs) exist in the live
  `item.kim`. The rule is: middle boss 70057 at 50 %, boss 70058 always, grade 3 70057
  always. That runs once per live party member (`DungeonRoom.cpp:6471`), and on fields at
  `BattleFieldRoom.cpp:2217/:5270`.
- **Boss ED piles:** the emulator drops coins (`Handlers_Room.cpp:2884` after the Phase 2 review, 2-3 per kill, ED on
  pickup). Make that 10-20 for grade 1, and correct the stale notes in
  `X2OfflineDropTable.h:99-109`: coins are dropped, and the factors do not fold into items.
- **Kill-all rule: not ported.** The server's check can only refuse more than the emulator's
  `true`, and an honest client always passes.

**Gate.**
- A dungeon boss kill logs factor 12, with EXP = base × 12 × boost.
- A middle boss kill logs factor 4.
- The special drop appears, or its absence is logged.
- A normal kill is unchanged.
